# Product spec

## What this is

sergioestrella.com is the personal website of Sergio Estrella, also known online as Djkde, a fullstack developer and community organizer. It holds a blog in Spanish and a page about his work.

## Why it exists

It gives Sergio one place to publish what he learns, share his talks and show his experience.

## Who it is for

- **Readers of the blog:** people in the Spanish-speaking tech community who want event recaps, talks and career advice.
- **People checking his background:** recruiters, potential clients and event organizers who want to see what he has done.
- **Sergio himself:** he writes posts as Markdown files and publishes them without running a content system.

## What it does today

- A home page with a hero, his current focus, metrics and the three latest posts.
- An about page with his bio, contact links, current stack, work experience and projects.
- A paginated blog, category pages and related posts.
- An RSS feed, a sitemap, and metadata for search engines and social sharing.

## Where it is going

The site should become a more editorial portfolio that tells a stronger story about his work, talks, community and clients. The phases are in [portfolio-roadmap.md](portfolio-roadmap.md).

## What success looks like

- Every published post appears on the home page or blog, in its category and in RSS.
- A new post takes one Markdown file and no other change.
- Reading stays comfortable on a phone, with nothing moving under the text.
- New work does not lower the site's Lighthouse scores against the previous baseline.

## Non-goals

- A content management system or a database.
- Comments, accounts or anything else that needs a server.
- Content in languages other than Spanish.
- A full visual rewrite. The roadmap adds to the current design rather than replacing it.
